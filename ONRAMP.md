## 4. Swap once a month: the arithmetic

Take the measured cost of a $1 swap and of a $100 swap (USDC-BASE -> XNO, vs CoinGecko) and apply them to a monthly budget. Nano payments between agents carry no fee, so the swap is the only conversion cost; the calls themselves cost nothing extra. These products are computed from the table, not quoted by the service for those sizes except the $1, $10, $100 and $1000 rows.

| Monthly budget | In $1 swaps | In $10 swaps | One $100 swap per month (if budget is $100) | One swap for the whole budget |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $10 | $0.75 (10 swaps x 7.52%) | $0.18 (1 swaps x 1.78%) | n/a | $0.18 (1.78%) |
| $50 | $3.76 (50 swaps x 7.52%) | $0.89 (5 swaps x 1.78%) | n/a | no measured $50 quote; between the $10 and $100 rows |
| $100 | $7.52 (100 swaps x 7.52%) | $1.78 (10 swaps x 1.78%) | $1.02 | $1.02 (1.02%) |

So a $100 month done as one swap lost about $1.02 against $7.52 when done as $1 swaps: the same money, 7.4 times the loss. Rules that follow:

1. **Never swap per call or per day below about $10.** Below it the fixed part of the quote dominates (the $1 row).
2. **Swap the month's budget once**, to XNO, and hold the float in XNO. Every payment from the float is feeless. Price risk of holding XNO for a month is yours and is not in these tables; decide how much to hold on that basis.
3. **Only convert back what you must.** The round trip costs the sum of both legs (table 1, last column): about 5% at $100 and more below $10. If a counterparty will take XNO directly, there is no second leg (see https://github.com/dhyabi2/xno-acceptance-registry).
4. **Re-quote immediately before swapping.** Every figure is one snapshot; the quote and limits move.
